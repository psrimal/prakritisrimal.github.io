Background

The path here

01 / 05
Chapter 01
2018
I studied architecture.
I fell in love with the space between buildings.
B.Arch at BMS College of Engineering. Five years learning how to design — but the thing that held me was what happened outside the building. How people moved. Why some streets felt alive and others didn't. Urban design was the answer.
BMS College · Bangalore
Chapter 02
2022
A thesis about fifteen minutes.
A city as a machine.
MArch Urban Design at UCL Bartlett's B-Pro program, London. My thesis — IsoChronic City — explored post-pandemic reurbanisation through 15-minute neighbourhood principles, machine learning, and generative design. That's when it clicked.
UCL Bartlett · London
Chapter 03
2023
They asked me to join an automation team.
We were all architects writing Python.
Joined a computational design team. Ended up writing Python for Revit with zero prior coding background. pyRevit, Revit API, workflow automation. The tools were unfamiliar. The spatial logic was not.
AEC Automation · Revit API
Chapter 04
Now
Bangalore as a case study.
Spatial data as a language.
Urban Data Scientist at UIL R&D. Spatial analysis, machine learning, real-time sensing. Building instruments from scratch. Writing code that reads the city. The tools I know are not enough yet. That's the point.
UIL R&D · Bangalore
