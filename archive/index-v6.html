The path here

2018
B.Arch — BMS College of Engineering
Architecture degree. Fell in love with urban design — the life inside the building, not the building itself.
2022
MArch Urban Design — UCL Bartlett
B-Pro program, London. Thesis: IsoChronic City — 15-minute neighbourhoods via ML and generative design. That's when it clicked.
2023
AEC Automation — pyRevit + Python
Joined a computational design team as an architect. Ended up writing Python for Revit with zero prior coding background.
Now
Urban Data Scientist — UIL R&D
Spatial analysis, ML, real-time sensing. Bangalore as a case study. The tools I know are not enough yet. That's the point.

I've always wanted to design for people. The smallest things have the biggest impact.

Trained as an architect, I ran away from coding my entire life — then got a call to join an automation team. We were all architects writing Python. It stuck.

Now I apply the same spatial rigour to data: geospatial pipelines, machine learning models, and hardware sensing instruments built from scratch. Bangalore is my current case study — a rapidly growing city with vast amounts of unread spatial data.
